global defense • vishesh report

duniya ke 10 sabse vinashkari aur ghatak hathiyar

aadhunik yuddh ke maidan ki disha badalne wale, 10 sabse shaktishali sainya hathiyaron ki vistrit jankari.

1. zaar bomb (Tsar Bomba) – itihas ka sabse shaktishali hydrogen bomb

soviet sangh dwara 1961 mein parikshan kiya gaya yeh bomb ab tak ka sabse bada parmanu hathiyar hai. iski kshamta 50 megaton thi, jo hiroshima par giraye gaye bomb se 3,300 guna adhik takatvar tha.

mukhya visheshatayen:
  • visphot ka mashroom cloud 64 km uncha utha tha.
  • iski shockwave ne dharti ke teen chakkar lagaye the.

2. rs-28 sarmat (RS-28 Sarmat / Satan II) – super-heavy ICBM

roos ki yeh antarmahadweepiya ballistic missile duniya ki sabse bhari aur khatarnak missilon mein gini jati hai, jo ek sath kai parmanu hathiyar le ja sakti hai.

mukhya visheshatayen:
  • range 18,000 kilometer se adhik hai.
  • yeh desh ke ek bade hisse ko pal bhar mein mita sakti hai.

3. avangard hypersonic glide vehicle – dhwani se 27 guna tez

roos ki yeh hathiyar pranali dhwani ki gati se 20 se 27 guna adhik gati se yatra karti hai aur raste mein apni disha badal sakti hai.

mukhya visheshatayen:
  • ise track karna kisi bhi radar ke liye lagbhag asambhav hai.
  • yeh duniya ke kisi bhi defense shield ko bhed sakti hai.

4. ohio-class submarine – samandar ki adrishya shakti

amriki nausena ki yeh parmanu panadubbiyan mahasagaron mein mahino tak chipkar 24 trident parmanu missilein le jane ki kshamta rakhti hain.

mukhya visheshatayen:
  • pani ke bhitar se hi duniya ke kisi bhi kone par hamla kar sakti hai.
  • atyant shant aur radar ki pakad se bahar rahne wali taknik.

5. posaydan (Poseidon Nuclear Torpedo) – parmanu tsunami drone

yeh roos ka ek manvarahit panadubbiyon wala drone hai, jise tatiy shahron aur bandargahon ko radiodharmi tsunami se nasht karne ke liye design kiya gaya hai.

mukhya visheshatayen:
  • samudra ke bhitar bahut gahrai mein chupchap yatra karta hai.
  • visphot se vishalkay radiodharmi lehrein uthti hain.

6. b-2 spirit bomber (Stealth Bomber) – adrishya vidhwansak

amrika ka yeh flying-wing design wala bomber paramparik aur parmanu dono tarah ke hathiyaron ko dushman ke radar ki nazaron se bachakar girane mein mahir hai.

mukhya visheshatayen:
  • atyant unnat stealth taknik jo radar ko chakma deti hai.
  • bina ruke duniya mein kahin bhi jakar hamla karne ki kshamta.

7. minuteman-iii (Minuteman III) – bhumi aadharit missile

yeh amriki vayu sena ki mukhya bhumi-aadharit antarmahadweepiya ballistic missile hai, jo silo (underground base) se kuch hi minaton mein launch ki ja sakti hai.

mukhya visheshatayen:
  • lagbhag 24,000 km/ghanta ki teevr gati.
  • atyant sateek nishana lagane ki kshamta.

8. dongfeng-41 (DF-41 ICBM) – chin ki samarik takat

chin ki yeh road-mobile ICBM duniya ki sabse lambi duri tak mar karne wali missilon mein se ek hai, jise truck par rakhkar kahin bhi le जाया ja sakta hai.

mukhya visheshatayen:
  • lagbhag 12,000 se 15,000 km tak ki marak kshamta.
  • road-mobile hone ke karan ise satellite se khojna kathin hai.

9. brahmos (Supersonic Cruise Missile) – bharat ki shaan

bharat aur roos dwara sanyukt roop se viksit **brahmos** duniya ki sabse tez aur behtareen cruise missilon mein se ek hai, jo zamin, hawa aur pani teeno jagahon se chhodi ja sakti hai.

mukhya visheshatayen:
  • yeh mach 2.8 se 3 ki supersonic gati se udti hai.
  • 'fire and forget' taknik par kaam karti hai.

10. f-35 lightning ii (Stealth Fighter) – panchwein peedhi ka jet

amrika ka yeh fighter jet hawa mein dushman ke vimanon ko chakma dene aur unnat radar v sensor se lais hokar sateek bombबारी karne ke liye vishwarbhar mein prasiddh hai.

mukhya visheshatayen:
  • atyant unnat data networking aur sensor fusion.
  • dushman ke air defense ko bhedne mein poori tarah saksham.
