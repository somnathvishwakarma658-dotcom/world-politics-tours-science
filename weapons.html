Duniya Ke 5 Sabse Ghatak Hathiyaar

Heading par click karke har hathiyar ki vistrit jankari dekhein.

Yeh insani itihasdwara banaye gaye sabse vinashkari hathiyaar hain. Yeh kisi bhi pure shahar ko ek hi jhatke mein puri tarah tabah karne ki kshamta rakhte hain.

Pramukh Udaharan: Russia ka Tsar Bomba, jo ab tak ka sabse shaktishali visphot karne wala parmanu hathiyaar hai.

Tabaahi Ki Kshamta: Iska visphot lakho degree celsius ka tapman paida karta hai aur kai sau kilometer ke kshetra mein sab kuch rakh kar deta hai.

Yeh aisi balistic missiles hain jo ek mahadweep (Continent) se dusre mahadweep tak behad kam samay mein parmanu hathiyaar pahuncha sakti hain.

Pramukh Udaharan: Russia ki RS-28 Sarmat (Satan II) aur USA ki Minuteman III.

Raftaar Aur Range: Inki range 10,000 km se bhi zyada hoti hai aur yeh antariksh (space) se hokar apne target par hamla karti hain.

Hypersonic missiles dhwani ki gati (speed of sound) se 5 guna (Mach 5) ya usse bhi zyada tezi se udti hain.

Pramukh Udaharan: Russia ki Zircon aur Kinzhal missiles.

Khasiyat: Yeh na sirf behad tezi se chalti hain balki hawa mein apna rasta bhi badal sakti hain, jisse ise kisi bhi radar ya air defense system dwara rokna namumkin jaisa ho jata hai.

Samundar ke gahrayi mein mahino tak bina bahar aaye chipi rehne wali yeh pandubbiya chalti-phirti tabahi ka kendra hain.

Pramukh Udaharan: USA ki Ohio-Class aur Russia ki Borei-Class submarines.

Khasiyat: Inme se ek hi submarine ke paas itni nuclear missiles hoti hain ki woh pure ek desh ko akele hi tabah kar sakti hai.

Aadhunik yuddh mein Artificial Intelligence (AI) aur Autonomous Drones bina kisi insani madad ke khud target dhundhkar hamla kar sakte hain.

Pramukh Udaharan: USA ka MQ-9 Reaper aur Drone Swarms (ek sath sekdo drones ka hamla).

Khasiyat: Yeh bina kisi pilot ke dushman ke ilaqe mein ghuskar sateek hamla karne aur bhavishya ke yuddh ka rasta badalne mein saksham hain.
